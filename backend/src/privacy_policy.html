Introduction

Welcome to Expiry Scanner ("App," "we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our App. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights regarding your data.

Information We Collect

Product Information

  • Product Data: Product names, barcodes, expiration dates, and quantities
  • Product Images: Photographs of products you add to the App
  • User Preferences: Your custom product information and settings
  • Inventory Data: Stock levels, freezer quantities, and storage locations

Barcode Database

The App maintains a barcode database to help identify products automatically. This database is used only to enhance your experience by suggesting product information when you scan barcodes.

User Account Information

  • Client ID: A unique identifier (UUID) assigned to your account
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the App
  • Timestamps: When products are created, modified, or frozen

Automatically Collected Information

  • Application crash logs and error reports
  • Device information (platform: iOS, macOS, or Android)
  • IP address (for backend API communication)
Note: We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location, unless you explicitly provide it to enhance your account experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the App's core functionality
  • To help you track product expiration dates and inventory
  • To identify products based on barcode scanning
  • To store and retrieve your product information
  • To perform automated maintenance and cleanup tasks (removing stale or orphaned data)
  • To improve and optimize the App's performance
  • To debug and resolve technical issues
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Storage & Security

Where Your Data is Stored

All your product data, images, and user preferences are stored on our secure backend database server. Your Client ID is used as the unique identifier to ensure your data remains separate from other users' data.

Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Database encryption and access controls
  • CORS (Cross-Origin Resource Sharing) policies to restrict unauthorized access
  • TLS/SSL encryption for data in transit
  • Regular database backups
  • Restricted API access to authorized applications only
Important: While we take security seriously, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong practices when accessing the App on shared devices.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal product data with third parties.

Your data may only be accessed or shared in the following limited circumstances:

  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, court order, or government request
  • Service Providers: We may use third-party services for infrastructure hosting and database management. These providers are contractually bound to maintain confidentiality.
  • Data Aggregation: We may use anonymized, aggregated data for analytics and improvement purposes

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: You can access, review, and export all your data from within the App
  • Right to Modification: You can edit or update your product information at any time
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete individual products or your entire account and all associated data
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request a copy of your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided in the Contact Us section.

Data Retention

Active Data

Your product data and images are retained as long as your account remains active.

Automatic Cleanup

The App performs automated nightly maintenance tasks that:

  • Remove stale or expired stock entries after a certain period
  • Delete orphaned product information without associated inventory
  • Clean up broken user-product relationships
  • Refresh user access links for security purposes

Account Deletion

When you delete your account, all your product data, images, and preferences are permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. Please note that this action is irreversible.
